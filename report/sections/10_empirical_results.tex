\section{Empirical Results and Performance Analysis}
\label{sec:empirical_results}

The MedRAGAgents framework was evaluated across all four progressive variants (V0 through V3) on the official MedQA-USMLE benchmark test set. Evaluation scripts (\texttt{evaluate.py}) computed classification accuracy, invalid response rates, non-parametric 95\% bootstrap confidence intervals, and paired McNemar significance metrics.

\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Substantial Accuracy Gains}: Integrating multi-agent decomposition and MedCPT retrieval produced a net accuracy gain of $\Delta \text{Acc}(V_3, V_0) = +0.3000$ over the direct baseline.
\end{enumerate}
